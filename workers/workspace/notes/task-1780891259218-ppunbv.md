**Value anchor (D) — deliberately omitted as a comp.** The data does not support an honest comp:
every sold comp is 2+ acres while this lot is 0.12 acres. A per-acre comparison ($1,500 / 0.12 ac
≈ $12,500/ac vs ~$2,000–$3,000/ac for the 2-ac comps) would make the lot look *expensive* per acre
and a whole-price comp ("nearby sold for $6,000") compares unlike sizes. Per the scan-test clarity
rule, an unclear or misleading anchor is worse than none, so the value signal is carried as the
absolute low price in the offer clause ($1,500 / $99 down) instead. This is the standard move for a
sub-$2k lot. **Missing input that would strengthen future ads:** the county assessed value for this
specific APN, or a recent sold comp of a similarly small (<0.25 ac) Montello lot.

All headlines 90–100% of cap (landmodo at the 60-char cap keeps lead + standout + offer, drops the
non-existent comp; land_century at 92% is the only sub-95% — it cannot absorb another grounded
