## DESCRIPTION (1998/2000 chars)
Picture Saturday starting with the drive up from Denver and ending by a campfire under your own pines, on an acre with your name on the deed. It sits at 9,600 feet in Redhill Forest near Fairplay, Colorado. Marketplace has no land category, so it is posted as an item. A nearby 1 acre lot sold for $150,000. This one is $40,999.

What you get:

- 1 acre, Lot 366, Redhill Forest Filing 3, Park County, Colorado (parcel number R0037546)
- Forested, about 9,600 feet up, with the Mosquito Range across the valley
- Zoned Residential, so you can build a cabin or a home
- Legal access on association roads, graded and plowed year-round, so you get in during ski season too
- Power from CORE Electric along Redhill Road, so a cabin here is not a generator-only life
- Community water through the homeowners association (no private wells; the tap fee is substantial) and septic permits through Park County
- Association dues about $1,250 a year

The price:

- $40,999 cash, or
- $44,999 financed: $12,000 down plus a $249 document fee, then $1,375 a month for 24 months, $917 for 36 months, or $688 for 48 months

No credit check, no prepayment penalty, and 10% off the balance if you pay it off within 12 months. The deed records in your name at payoff, and you can camp on the lot while you pay, May 1 to October 31.

Where it is: Fairplay 11 miles, Breckenridge 33 miles over Hoosier Pass, Denver 93 miles. Weekends here mean fly fishing the South Platte (owners get private water on 5.5 miles), skiing Breckenridge, and South Park City's 1880s buildings.

Walk it yourself first: open the LandGlide app, search R0037546, tap the outlined parcel, turn on location, and the blue dot shows where you stand against the lot lines.

Confirm the water tap fee and dues with the association before you buy, since they set what a build costs you.
