# Photo shot list: get from 14 to 22+

Coach asked for at least 22 photos. The task has 14 (11 ground shots from October 2018, 3 map views). Everything below is a public place within an hour of Lot 366 that you can photograph yourself or source a licensed image of. Use your own photos or images with a clear license (Wikimedia Commons CC, CPW/USFS public domain, or a paid stock license). Do not lift listing photos from other Redhill Forest lots.

Priority order (closest and most on-message first):

1. **Redhill Forest entrance sign and cluster mailboxes** off CO-9 near Garo. Shows the subdivision is real and maintained.
2. **Trout Creek Ln looking toward the lot** in summer, showing the graded road. Pairs with the "plowed and graded" claim. Reshoot the lot too: the 2018 ground shots show snow patches; a green-season set would help.
3. **Middle Fork of the South Platte on the HOA fishing easement** (owner access; the HOA map shows areas 1 to 5). Sells the private fly fishing line in every variant.
4. **Mt Silverheels from CO-9 or Redhill Rd** (13,829 ft, the peak directly across the valley to the northwest).
5. **Mosquito Range from Fairplay Beach** (Lincoln, Democrat, Bross above Alma). A single frame of the range with the pond in front.
6. **South Park City museum, Front Street, Fairplay** (43 original 1880s buildings). Public attraction, easy to shoot from the street.
7. **Fairplay Beach pond and gold-panning area** on the Middle Fork.
8. **Tomahawk State Wildlife Area** sign or river bend on CO-9 between Garo and Hartsel (1,655 acres, public fishing).
9. **Rocky Mountain Land Library at Buffalo Peaks Ranch, Garo**, the historic ranch buildings right by the subdivision entrance.
10. **Hoosier Pass summit sign** (11,542 ft, Continental Divide, on the way to Breckenridge).
11. **Breckenridge Main Street or the ski runs from CO-9** (33 road miles).
12. **Kenosha Pass aspen overlook** in late September (the view down into South Park on the Denver route).
13. **Antero Reservoir** with the Buffalo Peaks behind it (16 road miles).
14. **Spinney Mountain Reservoir or Eleven Mile Reservoir** shoreline with the Collegiate Peaks on the horizon.
15. **Alma, CO town sign** ("highest incorporated town in North America", 16 road miles).
16. **Limber Grove bristlecone pines** on the trail off Fourmile Rd outside Fairplay, if you want a tree-themed shot that matches "forested".

Also worth adding from your own screen:

17. **LandGlide screenshot** with R0037546 highlighted and the blue location dot on the lot. Backs the step-by-step section.
18. **Park County zoning map screenshot** (maps.parkco.us) showing the parcel in the R zone.
19. **A plat crop of Redhill Forest Filing 3** with Lot 366 circled (county recorder or the HOA lot map at redhillforesthoa.com/lot-map).
