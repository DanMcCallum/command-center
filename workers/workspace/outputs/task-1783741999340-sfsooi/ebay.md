WHAT IS BLM LAND?

BLM stands for the Bureau of Land Management. It is the federal agency that looks after public land across the western states, and land it manages belongs to the public: you can generally hunt it, hike it, ride it, and camp on it under the agency's rules without owning an acre of it yourself. That is what public land nearby means on this listing. Public land also stays public, so open country near you is not going to get subdivided out from under you.

THE DEED

You get a warranty deed. A warranty deed means the seller guarantees the title, so we stand behind it against any claim on the property that goes back before your purchase, including years we had nothing to do with. A special warranty deed only covers the stretch the seller owned it, and a quitclaim deed guarantees you nothing at all, so of the three this is the one you want.
