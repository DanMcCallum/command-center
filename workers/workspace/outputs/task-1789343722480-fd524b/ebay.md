THE DEED

You get a warranty deed. A warranty deed means the seller guarantees the title all the way back through its history, not only for the years the seller owned it, and stands behind that guarantee if anything ever surfaces.

That matters because of what the other two deeds do. A quitclaim deed hands over whatever interest the seller happens to have and guarantees nothing. A special warranty deed covers only the seller's own years of ownership. Plenty of cheap land online moves on those two.

We pay the recording fee and the transfer tax. On a land contract the deed records in your name at payoff, so while the payments are running the title stays in our name and you hold the contract.
