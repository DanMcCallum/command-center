Note for the operator: the E (Eliminate Objections) passes are carried by financing clarity, the APN-verification invitation, and the offer to send photos and the map location. None of the seven variants can answer the access question ("is it landlocked, what road do I take") because the task metadata has no access field. Supplying that one fact would make E stronger on every platform. See `notes.md`.

The eBay variant passes E on a different footing: seller-terms supplies the deed type, the guarantee, the payment methods, the pre-screen rule, and the deadline, and the listing states plainly which parcel facts are unverified rather than filling them in. That honesty is what carries the block; it is not a substitute for getting the access and utility facts.
