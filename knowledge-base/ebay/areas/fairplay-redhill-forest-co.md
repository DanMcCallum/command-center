## Usable now

### Where it is
- Redhill Forest is a covenant-controlled subdivision on the ridge between Fairplay (north) and Hartsel (south), east of Colorado Highway 9. 583 lots of 1 to 9 acres; over 150 homes, about 40 of them primary residences. Elevation 9,200 ft at the entrance to almost 10,000 ft. (redhillforesthoa.com, home page, read 2026-10-02)
- Entrance and mailboxes are off Highway 9 near Garo. Internal roads: Redhill Rd, Bonell Dr, Trout Creek Ln, Middle Fork Vista, Crooked Creek Rd. (county parcel data + HOA site)
- Park County zoning for Redhill Forest Filing 3 lots is R (Residential). (Park County assessor parcel export, `land-comps/data/county/park/parcels.csv`)
- Elevation at Lot 366, Filing 3 (R0037546): about 9,580 ft. (USGS EPQS, 2026-10-02)

### Roads and drive distances (road miles from Filing 3, OSRM routing 2026-10-02; round in copy)

### Utilities
- HOA annual assessment, Fee Schedule 2024: $1,025 unimproved lot, $1,250 improved lot. Ads written before 2026-10-18 say "about $1,250 a year per recent listings"; for a bare lot use $1,025. (HOA fee schedule PDF, read 2026-10-18)
- Electric: CORE Electric Cooperative (formerly Intermountain Rural Electric Association). Standard service 200 amp; CORE accepts up to 10 kW grid-tied solar. Listings describe power as running along Redhill Rd; confirm distance to the pole for a specific lot. (HOA "Living" page; listing copy)
- Water: HOA community water system (rebuilt 2012 to 2014, lines buried 9+ ft, freeze-proof). Private wells are not permitted. Fee Schedule 2024 (v1.4, Jan 2024, linked from the HOA "Water" page; copy in `operator-input/facebook-inquiry-2026-10-13/`): water tap fee $7,500 (non-refundable, non-transferable), $10,000 excavation warranty bond, one-time Utility Service Availability Fee $1,650 for Filings 1 to 3 (lots 1 to 377; $2,420 Filing 4, $3,300 Filing 5), monthly base $70 up to 3,000 gallons then $0.02/gal to 5,000 (max $40) and $0.10/gal above. The HOA's rule of thumb is "$17,500+" to bring water onto the lot: the tap plus about $10,000 for the pit and road cut, plus "$45+ per foot" for the line to the structure. Domestic use only, no lawns. (HOA "Water" and "Building" pages, fee schedule PDF, read 2026-10-18) In copy: "tap fee $7,500 on the HOA's 2024 schedule, budget $17,500+ all in, confirm with the association".
- Sewer: septic, permitted through Park County. HOA RV dump station south of the entrance mailboxes, $45 key charge. (HOA "Living" page)
- Trash: Mountain View Waste Systems, weekly Monday pickup, bear-proof cans available. Mail: cluster boxes at the entrance via Fairplay post office; UPS and FedEx deliver. (HOA "Living" page)
- Phone/internet: CenturyLink landline only (no high-speed data). Cell: AT&T and Verizon good on the west ridge, poor on the east ridge; T-Mobile poor overall. Internet by point-to-point wireless, satellite, or Starlink. (HOA "Living" page)

### HOA rules that affect use
- Camping season May 1 to October 31. Camping is for owners only, on approved surfaces or pads. Stays over 14 days need a Park County camping permit (submitted to the HOA first) and HOA approval. RVs may not be stored on a vacant lot outside the season. Fire rings need a NW Fire burn permit. (HOA "Fish/Hunt/Camp" page)
- Park County, for parcels 1 to 19.99 acres: 2 camping units allowed; 14 days without a permit; 30 days with a permit plus a 30-day extension; up to 90 days with a permit only with well and septic. (Park County camping regulations, 2021)
- Any improvement needs an Architectural Committee form through the KC & Associates owner portal. Tree removal needs a form and site inspection. Standalone sheds, tiny homes, and camping cabins are not permitted. (HOA "Building" page)
- Management: KC & Associates, 10106 W. San Juan Way, Suite 210, Littleton, CO 80127, 303-933-6279, kathy.christensen@kchoa.com. (HOA "Management/Board" page)

### What you see and do
- Lot owners get private fly fishing on approximately 5.5 miles of the Middle Fork of the South Platte through two exclusive easements; Gold Medal water, catch and release, fly rods and barbless hooks only. Areas 1 to 3 members only; Area 4 allows two guests; Area 5 is reserved for specific lots. (HOA "Fish/Hunt/Camp" page)
- Fairplay: South Park City open-air museum (about 43 original 1880s buildings, 60,000+ artifacts); Fairplay Beach pond on the Middle Fork with gold panning and trout fishing; Front Street historic district; Burro Days (late July). (tripadvisor/uncovercolorado/southparkcity)
- Peaks across the valley: Mt Silverheels (13,829 ft), Mt Lincoln, Mt Democrat, Mt Bross (fourteeners above Alma), Boreas Mountain. Hiking: Mt Silverheels, Limber Grove bristlecone trail, Tie Hack trail. (search results 2026-10-02)
- Skiing: Breckenridge over Hoosier Pass about 1 hr; Copper, Keystone, A-Basin 60 to 85 min. (skyfall-chalet.com drive-times page, a rental inside Redhill Forest)
- Fishing and water: Antero, Spinney Mountain, and Eleven Mile reservoirs (Gold Medal waters); Tomahawk State Wildlife Area on the South Platte along Highway 9 between Garo and Hartsel (1,655 acres); Rocky Mountain Land Library at Buffalo Peaks Ranch at Garo. (CPW, Colorado Encyclopedia)
- Public land: Pike National Forest to the north and east of South Park; three wilderness areas and 12 state wildlife areas in Park County. (exploreparkcounty.com)
